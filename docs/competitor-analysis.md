# Competitor & Audience Intelligence — Research Plan

This file is a framework, not a claim that current channel metrics have been collected.

## Creator / format groups to study

- Internet commentary: penguinz0, Atozy.
- Comedy commentary: Danny Gonzalez, Drew Gooden, Kurtis Conner.
- Viral story narration: Ray William Johnson.
- Pop-culture commentary: Film Cooper.
- Topical satire: Saturday Night Live Weekend Update, The Daily Show.
- Satirical headlines: The Onion.

## What to record

For each public video/post, record:
- creator and platform
- publication date
- subject / story
- format and duration
- views and engagement observed on the date of research
- opening device
- central comedic premise
- joke density and escalation pattern
- audience comments: what viewers quote, praise, dispute or ask for
- underserved angle we could explore

## Audience opportunity

Use competitor coverage as demand discovery. Do not copy the competitor's script, jokes, title framing, thumbnail, persona or distinctive presentation. Find a new observation and add value to the public conversation.

## Research caution

Views and subscriber counts change quickly and third-party rankings may use different methods. Keep dated observations and compare like with like.
