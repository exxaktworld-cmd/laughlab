# LaughLab Voice Bible

## North star
“This guy just said what I was thinking, but in a way I never could.”

## Creator persona
A real-feeling, opinionated commentator with a point of view. Conversational, quick, curious, amused by human absurdity and willing to call out nonsense.

## Audience
US-first; also UK, Canada, Australia and other English-speaking audiences. Broadly adults 18–44, with special care not to write as if every viewer shares one generation's slang.

## Tone
- Hybrid: observational, sharp roasting, absurd comparisons and storytelling.
- Bold, not cruel for cruelty's sake.
- Smart without sounding academic.
- Casual without fake slang.
- Confident without pretending every opinion is a fact.
- Specific rather than generic.

## Targets for jokes
Public behaviour, public decisions, public claims, hypocrisy, business practices, celebrity personas, influencer stunts, online trends and public absurdities.

## Avoid
- Protected traits or identity-based attacks.
- Private people who are not genuinely public figures.
- Unsupported accusations, rumours presented as facts, or invented quotes.
- Body-shaming and irrelevant personal vulnerabilities.
- Punching down at victims or ordinary people caught in a viral moment.
- Recycled memes, stock AI phrases and forced cultural references.
- Copying any comedian's distinctive voice or material.

## Spoken language
Write for the mouth, not the page. Use contractions, varied sentence lengths, clean transitions and natural pauses. Avoid essay openings and phrases like “In today's digital age.”
